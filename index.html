What to know
before applying.

Who is a strong fit?

Emerging models and creators with presence, ambition, and clear potential who want sharper branding, content, and direction.

Do I need a large following?

No. A smaller creator with strong presence, consistency, and a clear spark may be a better fit than a large but unfocused account.

What can you help me improve?

Your positioning, profile, visual direction, content systems, audience strategy, promotion, and path to better opportunities.

Will I have to change my image?

The goal is not to replace what makes you interesting. It is to sharpen it, package it, and make it easier for the right audience to understand.

Why is onboarding selective?

A focused roster means more attention, stronger collaboration, and a better chance to build something distinct instead of treating every creator the same.
